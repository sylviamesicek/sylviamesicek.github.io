    Hello! I am a post-baccalaureate researcher with honors degrees in Physics and Mathematics at the University of Utah. I am pursuing a career in computational astrophysics and have a particular fascination for gravitation and black hole physics.
    </p>

        <h2>Research</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="research-projects">
        <div class="research-contents">
            <h4><span class="icon"><i class="fas fa-star"></i></span> Numerical Relativity</h4>
            <p>I study theoretical black hole physics in the Department of Physics and Astronomy, working in Dr. John Belz's numerical relativity group. I use numerical    methods to explore "critical phenomena" occuring on the threshold of black hole formation. My work includes:
            <ul>
                <li>Developing an advanced codebase for adaptively solving PDEs in axisymmetry.</li>
                <li>Adapting the Einstein Field Equations into forms suitable for numerical integration.</li>
                <li>Analyzing simulations of black hole collapse.</li>
            </ul>
            </p>
